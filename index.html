Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Golden Order respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the application handles information.

Information Collection

Golden Order does not require users to create an account or sign in. The application does not collect personal information such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, contacts, photos, or location data.

Orders and Notifications

The application connects to its own online API to retrieve order data and application notifications. These notifications are generated by the application's backend service and displayed to the user.

Advertising

Golden Order does not display advertisements.

Third-Party Services

The application does not use Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, or advertising SDKs.

Data Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect communication between the application and its backend services.

Children's Privacy

The application is not specifically directed toward children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any future changes will be published on this page.

Contact

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the application developer.
